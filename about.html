01 — Manifesto

Most organisations juggle five vendors for one building. Annapoorna replaces the list with one accountable partner.

Who we are

Built around one idea

Cleaning chemicals, the pantry, housekeeping, pest control, plumbing, deep cleaning and greenery are usually five different phone calls. Annapoorna was founded to bring the essentials of facility management under one roof — run by people who answer when you call.

How we work

Local, on schedule

From our head office in JP Nagar, 6th Phase, Bengaluru, we deliver consumables on a schedule and dispatch trained crews when work is needed — for offices, institutions, apartments, hotels, hospitals, schools and industrial units across the city.
